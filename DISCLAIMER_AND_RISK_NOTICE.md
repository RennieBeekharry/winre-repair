# RescueMeAI™ Disclaimer, Assumption of Risk, and Recovery Notice

**Version:** 2026-08-14

RescueMeAI is computer-recovery software. Recovery work can alter low-level system state and can cause or worsen loss of access, data loss, corruption, downtime, or the need for reinstall or hardware service even when a proposed action is reasonable.

## Before using RescueMeAI

You should understand the following:

1. **No recovery outcome is guaranteed.** A diagnosis or repair can be incorrect, incomplete, or unsuccessful.
2. **Hardware faults can mimic software faults.** A system that appears repairable in software may have an underlying storage, memory, power, firmware, or other hardware problem.
3. **Write operations carry risk.** Driver changes, registry changes, servicing changes, boot changes, image operations, partition operations, resets, and similar actions can make a system less recoverable if they fail or target the wrong state.
4. **Backups matter.** Where reasonably possible, important files and recovery keys should be backed up before recovery writes are performed.
5. **Power loss and interruption matter.** Interrupting a write operation can leave the operating system or recovery media inconsistent.
6. **Encryption can make data inaccessible.** Do not proceed with an action that depends on an encryption recovery key unless the required key is available.
7. **AI can be wrong.** AI-assisted diagnosis does not replace validation, integrity checks, risk classification, or local human authorization.

## RescueMeAI safety boundary

RescueMeAI is intended to use least-destructive methods first and to preserve evidence and rollback paths where practical.

Remote authorization alone must not approve a destructive operation. High-impact operations are subject to a separate local authorization process. Acceptance of the Terms of Use does not authorize any specific destructive operation.
