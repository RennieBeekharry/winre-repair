## Acceptance

RescueMeAI requires affirmative local acceptance of the current Terms version before normal recovery operation begins. The user must type exactly `ACCEPT` when prompted.

General Terms acceptance does **not** authorize a destructive recovery action. Destructive actions remain subject to a separate, action-specific local authorization gate.

## Versioning

Material changes to the Terms or associated legal package should receive a new legal/Terms version so RescueMeAI can require renewed acceptance when appropriate.

## Legal review

The project legal documents are intended to establish RescueMeAI operating terms and risk disclosures. Before commercial public launch, the final legal package should be reviewed by qualified counsel for the jurisdictions in which RescueMeAI will be offered.
