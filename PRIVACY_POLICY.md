# RescueMeAI™ Privacy Policy

**Policy version:** 2026-08-14  
**Effective date:** August 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RescueMeAI handles information when used for computer diagnosis and recovery.

## 1. Privacy principle

RescueMeAI is designed to collect the minimum technical information reasonably necessary to diagnose, validate, execute, and audit recovery actions.

The project should prefer local processing and local logs whenever practical. Remote reporting must be limited to recovery-relevant technical evidence.

## 2. Information RescueMeAI may process

Depending on the feature used, RescueMeAI may process or record technical information such as:

- RescueMeAI version and command version;
- recovery stage, result, and return codes;
- operating-system edition, architecture, build, and recovery environment information;
- device, controller, driver, service, storage, boot, networking, and update metadata relevant to diagnosis;
- hardware identifiers needed to match drivers or determine compatibility;
- filesystem paths, package names, driver names, registry paths, service names, and configuration values relevant to recovery;
- network reachability, DNS, HTTPS, and service-response information;
- command IDs, risk classifications, integrity hashes, timestamps, and safety-gate outcomes;
- a randomly generated recovery-agent identifier used to target and audit the active recovery session;
- diagnostic and repair logs generated by operating-system or RescueMeAI tools.

## 3. Information RescueMeAI is not designed to collect as routine telemetry

RescueMeAI should not intentionally collect or upload the following unless a user explicitly supplies them for a specific recovery purpose:

- passwords;
- personal access tokens, API keys, or authentication secrets;
- BitLocker or other encryption recovery keys;
- payment-card or banking information;
- the contents of personal documents, photos, messages, or other personal files;
- browser history or unrelated application data.

If sensitive data is unexpectedly present in a diagnostic log, it should be minimized, redacted, or excluded from remote reporting where reasonably possible.

## 4. Local storage

During recovery, RescueMeAI may store logs, configuration, authorization state, safety journals, acceptance records, cached network addresses, and recovery evidence on the local recovery computer or designated recovery media.

During the current development workflow, the legacy compatibility location may be `C:\WinRERepair`. New production installations are intended to use `C:\RescueMeAI` or another clearly identified RescueMeAI data location.

## 5. Remote reporting and command services

If remote recovery reporting is enabled, RescueMeAI may send recovery-relevant technical reports to an authenticated backend selected for the recovery session.

During development, GitHub may be used as the authenticated evidence/control backend. A production RescueMeAI relay may replace GitHub later.

Remote reports should not contain authentication secrets. Authentication credentials used to communicate with a backend should be stored locally and should not be committed to public or private evidence repositories.

## 6. AI providers

If a RescueMeAI deployment is configured to send diagnostic information to an AI provider, recovery-relevant information may be transmitted to that provider to perform diagnosis or planning. The deployment should disclose the provider and data flow before enabling that integration.

RescueMeAI should minimize information sent to AI systems and should not intentionally send unrelated personal-file contents or secrets.

## 7. Third-party services

RescueMeAI may interact with third-party services such as Microsoft download/update services, GitHub, hardware vendors, or configured AI providers. Information sent to those services is also subject to their respective privacy policies and service terms.

## 8. Retention

Local recovery evidence may remain on the computer or recovery media until it is manually removed or removed by RescueMeAI housekeeping features.

Remote evidence retention depends on the configured backend and project retention settings. Development evidence stored in a private repository remains under the repository owner's control and can be deleted through that service.

RescueMeAI should retain only the evidence reasonably useful for recovery, security, auditing, troubleshooting, or product improvement.

## 9. Product-improvement telemetry

If future versions add product-improvement telemetry, it should be privacy-minimized and designed to avoid collecting personal-file contents. Telemetry that is not required for the immediate recovery should be disclosed separately and, where appropriate, made optional.

## 10. Security

RescueMeAI uses safeguards such as authenticated channels, integrity validation, allowlisted command types, local destructive-action authorization, and separation of secrets from public source code. No security control can guarantee absolute confidentiality, integrity, or availability.

## 11. User choices

A user may decline the Terms and stop before RescueMeAI begins recovery operations. Where the product offers optional remote reporting or optional telemetry, users should be given a meaningful choice consistent with the feature's technical requirements.

## 12. Access, correction, and deletion

For locally stored information, the user or system owner controls the recovery computer and recovery media. For remotely stored development evidence, the repository/backend owner can inspect and delete the stored evidence subject to the provider's capabilities and applicable law.

Production versions should provide a documented process for privacy requests where required by applicable law.

## 13. Children

RescueMeAI is system-administration and recovery software and is not directed to children.

## 14. Changes to this policy

Material privacy changes receive a new policy version and should be communicated with the corresponding RescueMeAI Terms update where appropriate.

## 15. Contact and legal review

Before commercial public launch, this Privacy Policy should be reviewed and completed with the operator's legal name, contact information, retention periods, production providers, and jurisdiction-specific privacy disclosures.

---

This document describes the intended RescueMeAI privacy model and is not a representation that every deployment automatically satisfies every privacy law in every jurisdiction.
